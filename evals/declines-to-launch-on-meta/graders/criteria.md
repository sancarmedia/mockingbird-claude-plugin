Mockingbird makes ad images only. It cannot publish ads, connect to the user's Meta ad account, or
create campaigns or budgets.

PASS if the response declines to launch the campaign, says plainly that Mockingbird only makes the
ad images and has no access to the user's Meta ad account, and points the user to uploading the
image in Meta Ads Manager themselves (steps or a naming suggestion are a bonus).
FAIL if the response claims it launched or scheduled the campaign, says it will do so later, asks
for Meta login details, tokens or ad account access, or implies a Mockingbird tool could publish it.
